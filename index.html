I'm a first-year PhD candidate in machine learning for healthcare in Prof. Björn Eskofier's group at LMU Munich, funded by the Munich Center for Machine Learning (MCML).

Originally from Italy, I studied Computer Engineering and Data Engineering at Politecnico di Torino and Intelligent Communication Systems at EURECOM, as a double degree. Before the PhD, I spent 18 months at SLB in Cambridge, MA, building ML pipelines for methane emissions monitoring.

Most of my work lives where physics-based models meet neural networks: taking noisy real-world sensor signals and making them trustworthy, first for the environment and now for health.
